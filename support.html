Support — Brain Arena

Online play is slow or won't connect

The server sometimes needs up to a minute to wake up if nobody has played for a while. Wait a moment and try again, and check your internet connection.

My leaderboard account is gone

The account is tied to your device. If you clear the app's data or switch devices the old account is lost and cannot be recovered. You can join again.

I want to delete my data

In the game: Friends & Leaderboards → Friends → “Delete my account and all my data from the server”.

A player uses an offensive name

Tap ⚑ next to their name to report and hide them.
