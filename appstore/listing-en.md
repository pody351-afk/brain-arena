**Name:** Brain Arena
**Subtitle:** Brain games to play with friends

**Promotional text:** Hundreds of levels, daily challenges and online matches with friends. Four quick games that train your brain.

**Description:**
Brain Arena is a collection of fast, fun brain games in Arabic.

• Speed Brain – quick math, color and size questions. Right answers add time.
• Pop – tap groups of matching candies to pop them. Bigger groups, bigger scores. Bombs and rocks included.
• Memory – tiles flash for a moment; remember them all. Each level gets harder.
• Word Builder – scrambled letters; build every hidden word before time runs out.

Features:
• 110 levels across 9 worlds, with stars and bosses.
• A new daily challenge every day, plus daily and weekly quests and achievements.
• Play a friend online with a room code and see their score live.
• Optional friends and leaderboards.
• No ads, no purchases, no email or phone number required.
• Solo play works offline.
